Who I Am

About Me

My name is Zebtony Divine Chidubem. I am 21 years old, originally from Imo State, Nigeria, and currently living in Hungary, where I am studying Computer Science Engineering at the University of Dunaújváros.

I consider myself an ambitious, hardworking and forward-thinking young man who believes in continuous learning, personal development and creating opportunities.

I enjoy exploring new ideas, meeting people, learning about different cultures and challenging myself to achieve greater things. Whether in the classroom, in business, or in my community, I bring commitment, energy and creativity to everything I do.

My journey has taken me from Nigeria to Kenya and now to Hungary — each country leaving me with new skills, perspectives and a broader understanding of the world.

My Journey

Educational Background

My educational path has crossed three countries — Nigeria, Kenya, and Hungary — each chapter shaping who I am and broadening the opportunities ahead of me.

Secondary School · Nigeria

Madonna Model Secondary School

Owerri, Imo State, Nigeria

My academic journey began here in Owerri. I completed my secondary education at Madonna Model Secondary School, where I developed a strong foundation in sciences, mathematics and the humanities, and began to discover my passion for technology.

Diploma Studies · Kenya

Airport Operations & Flight Operations

Kenya Aeronautical College (KAC), Nairobi, Kenya

Following secondary school, I pursued my strong interest in aviation at Kenya Aeronautical College in Nairobi, Kenya. I successfully obtained diplomas in both Airport Operations and Flight Operations, gaining hands-on knowledge of the aviation industry.

I also earned an internationally recognised certification from the International Air Transport Association (IATA) in Airport Operations — achieving this at just 18 years old.

Computer Science Engineering

University of Dunaújváros, Dunaújváros, Hungary

My passion for technology led me to Hungary, where I am currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science Engineering at the University of Dunaújváros.
